2. General

The protection of your personal data is important to us. We process personal data exclusively within the framework of the statutory provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

3. Contact

This website does not use any contact or registration forms and does not collect any personal data via the website. Contact is made exclusively on a voluntary basis by email or via Instagram – in such cases, the privacy policies of the respective provider apply.

4. Hosting and server log files

When this website is accessed, the web server (hosting provider) automatically collects technical access data (e.g. IP address, date/time, pages accessed, browser type). This data is required for the operation of the website. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in the safe and functional operation of the website).

5. Fonts

This website uses locally embedded fonts. No font files are loaded from external servers (e.g. Google Fonts) and no data is transmitted to third parties.

6. External links (e.g. Instagram)

This website contains links to external services, e.g. Instagram. If you follow such a link, the privacy policies of the respective provider apply. We have no influence over the data collected there.

7. Your rights

Within the framework of the statutory provisions, you have the following rights:

  • – Right of access to your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • – Right to rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • – Right to erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • – Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • – Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • – Right to object to the processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • – Right to withdraw a given consent (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

8. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. with the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg).

9. Contact for privacy questions

If you have any questions about the processing of your personal data, please contact: whyartcommunity@pm.me
