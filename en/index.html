Why Art?

"Imagination is more important than knowledge. For knowledge is limited to all we now know and understand, while imagination embraces the entire world, and all there ever will be to know and understand" ALBERT EINSTEIN

The Idea

Art as a space for the new

In the spirit of Einstein's quote, we understand art as a space in which something new and alive can come into being. Through art, individual expression becomes possible, as does the shared development of ideas and impulses, along with a connection from person to person. Through our artistic work, we want to contribute creatively to the shaping of the future and of society.

Encounter & Creation

A space of encounter

Why Art opens up a space of encounter for artists, in which exchange, joint creation and the development of projects are made possible. Our artistic commitment is meant to support real projects and humanitarian aid organisations, where people work for peaceful coexistence, for humanity and for education.
