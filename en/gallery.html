Why Art Winter Exhibition 2022

With this exhibition we supported the Georgian organisation Youth Society Parzival, which has founded an anthroposophical educational establishment for children from the region. The goal is a diverse range of educational opportunities in Georgian regions – the first school building was erected in the village of Matsevani near Tbilisi.
